Connects your phone to the internet through public VPN configurations
(VLESS, Trojan, Shadowsocks, VMess) using the bundled Xray core. Config lists are
downloaded from open repositories, verified by real downloads and stored in a pool of
working configs — the fastest one is always used first.

There are no servers of our own: no subscriptions, no analytics, no data sent to a
"vendor" service. The only network traffic is to public config lists and to the servers
listed in them.

Three actions:
• Connect — picks the fastest config from the pool, verifies it and brings up the tunnel.
• Quick search — finds one working VPN and remembers it.
• Build pool — refreshes all lists, pings and measures the speed of live configs.

Highlights:
• Speed, not ping, decides: the pool is sorted by KB/s.
• DPI bypass of the line (ciadpi) with automatic strategy selection for networks where a
  direct connection fails.
• Three built-in settings presets: "Standard" (with DPI bypass), "Wi-Fi" (fast measuring,
  no DPI) and "White lists" (low threshold, white-list sources); custom presets can be
  imported from .json.
• Tor fallback with bridges, enabled only when no VPN config answers. Bridges are tested
  and ranked by speed.
• Built-in single-tab browser that works only through Tor with bridges.
• Settings and the console can be exported to .json for troubleshooting.
• All cores (Xray, ByeDPI, Tor) run locally; no root required.

An open project with no developer: the app has no author or owner, the code is GPL-3.0 — fork it, rewrite it, ship your own build.
