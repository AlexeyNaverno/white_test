• Fixed: working configs are no longer marked as dead. A bare 204 answer (typical for whitelist-only channels that allow only Cloudflare and Google) now counts as proof the tunnel works instead of a failure; previously such a config was blacklisted and dropped from the pool. Short checks always include one such URL.
• New Working configs screen: the whole pool in one list, with per-entry ping, speed re-measure, removal and blacklisting. A bottom button blacklists everything that failed a check. Opens from settings and by tapping the pool card.
